# SITREP Claim Journal

A symptom and evidence journal for veterans preparing a VA disability claim.

This repo currently holds a clickable prototype (`index.html`). Open it in any browser, ideally on a phone. It runs on example data for a made-up veteran, and nothing you enter is saved.

## What the prototype covers

- **Today:** one-minute daily check-in (0 to 10 severity per condition, sleep, whether symptoms limited the day, notes), flare-up logging, and countdowns to key dates like the C&P exam and Intent to File expiration.
- **Log:** 30-day timeline of check-ins and flare-ups, filterable by condition.
- **Conditions:** 30-day severity chart, flare-up stats and tracking tips per condition.
- **Evidence:** locker grouped by condition with Need / Requested / Have status.
- **Export:** summary for a VSO or C&P exam, copyable as text (a PDF in the finished app).

## Principles

- An organizer, not legal or medical advice. Not affiliated with the VA.
- Privacy first: health data stays on the device by default. No selling data.
- Affordable and on the veteran's side.
